WHERE THINGS STAND

Four things worth knowing before you read further.

Pulse is a device that watches over bedridden patients in the hours between carer rounds. A CNN-LSTM pipeline reads facial pain expression over time, a separate pose model tracks movement out of bed, and planned environmental sensors will add a fuller read on patient state, so problems are caught continuously instead of at the next check.

01 · THE PROBLEM

Nobody is in the room at 3am.

Up to four hours can pass between rounds for a patient who can't call for help. We're aiming to close that gap to closer to forty-one seconds.

02 · THE APPROACH

Two branches: a face, or a body.

In bed, a blanket hides the body, so Pulse reads the face. Out of bed, a pose model reads 17 body keypoints instead. Environmental sensors, planned next, add context from the room itself for a fuller read on patient state.

A laptop, a webcam, and a CAD file.
