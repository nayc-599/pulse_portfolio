Hero intro: the aperture and the cube

An iris opens onto a cube built from the real face's own 576 grid cells, one dot per cell. The cube thinks for three heartbeats, each one a lub-dub that pumps outwards through it from the front face. Its heart quickens, it turns to face you, and its front face settles into the grid. Once flat, the real paint() from js/pixel-grid.js takes over, driven by the same resting loop as js/hero.js. Move your cursor after it settles to check the eyes track.

Your system has reduced motion switched on, so this preview shows the static resting face, as the live hero does.
