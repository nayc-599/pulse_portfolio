Hero intro: the dot sphere

Each sphere is built from the real face's own 576 grid cells, 24 meridians by 24 parallels, so every dot has exactly one home cell. Once flat, the real paint() from js/pixel-grid.js takes over, driven by the same resting loop as js/hero.js. Move your cursor after it settles to check the eyes track.

Background

Read this before judging light mode. The settled face below is the live renderer, unmodified. paint() lights cells in --amber #F5C86B whatever the background, and that is 1.39:1 against bone, against 11.0:1 on charcoal. The charcoal dots only exist during the intro. A light hero would need paint() to take an ink colour, which is a change to the component and out of scope here.
