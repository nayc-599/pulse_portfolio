Team

Five of us, one evening a week.

At the Monash DeepNeuron showcase, 7 October 2026

We meet one evening a week on Zoom, inside Monash DeepNeuron, Monash University's premiere team in Artificial Intelligence and Optimised Computing. Nobody here has built a medical device before, which is roughly the point: this is a place to learn in public, and Pulse is what we chose to learn on.
