Our first meeting was mostly about what Pulse should actually be.

We started broad: a camera that watches over older people at home and spots falls. By the end we had narrowed it to **bedridden patients**, at home or in hospital, who can't reach a call button when something is wrong. For them, the gap between check-ins is the problem.

The plan we left with:

- A camera at the bedside that watches the patient's face and body for signs of distress.
- An app that alerts a carer when it sees something.
- Start general (pain and discomfort) rather than picking one disease, and narrow down later.
- Demo it on a laptop camera first. Build a proper device later, with room for extra sensors like temperature.

None of us had built hardware before, so we were honest that the showcase would be software first.
