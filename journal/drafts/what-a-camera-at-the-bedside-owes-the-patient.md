Putting a camera next to someone's bed is a big ask, so we set some rules early, before we had any data.

- **Keep as little as possible.** Pulse isn't meant to record patients. Footage is only kept as long as it's needed for training, and the live system works on frames as they arrive.
- **Research only.** The data we can use is licensed for research, not commercial use. Pulse stays a research prototype, and we say so on every page.
- **Our faces, with consent.** Every face you see on this site is one of us, and we agreed to it.
- **Don't publish what isn't ours.** No frames, faces or per-person results from the dataset appear anywhere public.

Since then, these rules have shaped the product too. The dashboard has a privacy view that blurs the video unless a carer chooses to look, and each look is logged. And the device has a face, so it feels less like a camera watching and more like something there to help.
