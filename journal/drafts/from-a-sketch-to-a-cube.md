Kate designed the body of the Pulse device.

It's a small **cube** that sits on a bedside table, with a camera on the front and an **LCD screen that shows a simple face**. The face is there on purpose: a camera pointed at a bed can feel like surveillance, and a friendly face makes the device feel less like that.

A few decisions from her design review:

- A **lip** around the base, so the cube sits steadily on the table.
- An opening for the **power cable** at the back.
- A **power button**, added for safety, so the device can always be switched off by hand.
- Space inside sized around the real components, so the case fits the parts, not the other way round.

By the end of September the CAD was about 90% done, the components had arrived, and the next step was printing a small prototype and fitting the parts inside. Nageeb then joined to lead the hardware integration.
